My research interests lie primarily in tonal analysis, transformational theory, the history of music theory, popular music, and music in contemporary digital media. My work has been published in journals such as Music Theory Spectrum, Music Theory Online, Music Analysis, Music Theory & Analysis, The Journal of Music Theory Pedagogy, The Journal of Sound and Music in Games, and in collections including The Routledge Companion to Music Theory Pedagogy, The Oxford Handbook of Public Music Theory, and The Oxford Handbook of Sound and Music in Video Games. On this page, I have collected my publications and works in progress into three major research projects.
